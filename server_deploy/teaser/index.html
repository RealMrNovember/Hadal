// 01 · The world

Year 2249. The surface is dead.

The atmosphere turned toxic. Sunlight became lethal. The continents can no longer hold life. What remained of humanity retreated to the Hadal Zone, the deepest layer of the ocean, and sealed itself inside pressure domes eleven kilometres below the waves.

They were not the first to arrive. On the ocean floor lie the remains of a biomechanical civilisation that lived millions of years before us: temples, machines that still seem to breathe, soldiers known only as The Forgotten. Humanity woke them.

Somewhere at the centre of the map lies The Core. Nobody who has gone looking has come back with a straight answer.

// 02 · Gameplay

Build. Breathe. Descend.

Your base is your home and your identity: a ring of domes around a single reactor, growing outward as you survive. Every action is checked by the game server, so the world plays fair for everyone, and your base keeps working while you are away.

Base building & upgrades

Playable now

The Core Reactor sits at the centre; everything else is built on rings around it. Tap a glowing slot to build, tap a building to upgrade. No building can outlevel the reactor, so every step forward starts at the heart of the base.

  • Construction runs on the server clock, even when you are offline
  • Short waits are on us: the last five minutes finish free
  • Buildings keep producing at their old level while they upgrade

A survival economy

Partly playable

Six resources keep a colony alive. Oxygen always comes first: if it runs out, the base dies. Energy comes from the reactor, biomass from living organisms, titanium from your drones, and hadalite only from the deepest zones.

  • Oxygen
  • Energy
  • Food
  • Biomass
  • Titanium
  • Hadalite

Submarine expeditions

In development

Build submarines at the Submarine Dock, crew them with three heroes and send them into the dark: bioluminescent forests, sunken cities, old human laboratories, alien temples. In some zones you cannot switch on your lights. There is only sonar.

Alliances & PvP

Planned

Nobody survives the abyss alone. Alliances will share structures and build underwater cities together; rival domes will lay siege to one another; and every road leads to the fight for The Core at the centre of the world.

// 04 · Install

Installing the Android test build

The test build is not on Google Play yet, so Android installs it as a file. It takes about a minute.

  1. 01

    Check your phone

    You need Android 8.0 or newer and an internet connection. iPhone is not supported yet.

  2. 02

    Download the APK

    Open this page on your phone and tap the download button. Your browser may warn that this type of file can be harmful; choose Download anyway.

  3. 03

    Allow installs from this source

    Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, tap Settings and turn on “Allow from this source” for your browser, then go back.

  4. 04

    Install and open

    Tap Install, then Open. On the title screen, tap to start. A guest account is created for you automatically.

// 05 · FAQ

Questions from the surface

What kind of game is HADAL?

A base-building strategy MMO set at the bottom of the ocean. You grow a colony of domes around a reactor, manage the resources that keep it alive, and, as development continues, send submarines on expeditions and join alliances with other players.

When is it coming to the App Store and Google Play?

There is no release date yet. HADAL is in early access testing and the store versions will follow when the game is ready. Until then, the Android test build on this page is the way to play.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. HADAL is being built for both iOS and Android, but the current test build is Android only.

Does the test build cost anything?

No. The Android test build is free to download and play.

Will my progress carry over to the full release?

We can't promise that. This is a test build, and progress may be reset as the game changes before launch.

How do I keep my account if I change phones?

Your guest account lives on your device. In the game, open Profile & Account and tap Protect Account: choose a password and you get a transfer code. On a new phone, use Sign In to Another Account with that code and password. Google and Apple sign-in are coming later.

Can I play offline?

You need a connection to play, because the game server runs the world. Your base keeps going without you, though: construction finishes and production continues while you are away.

Why does Android warn me about the APK?

Android shows a warning for any app installed outside an app store. The HADAL test build is published by Cicibyte; download it only from this site.
